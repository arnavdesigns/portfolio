About

02 / PROFILE

I'm Arnav, an interaction design student learning to think in states, transitions, and timing instead of static screens. Most of what's here is coursework and self-directed practice, not client work — I'm still early, and still figuring a lot of this out.

Before a pixel is placed, I try to ask what a person is actually doing — reaching, waiting, deciding — and design the response to match. Most of my learning happens in prototypes, not documents.

  • 01
    Motion has meaningEvery transition explains a change in state — nothing moves without a reason.
  • 02
    Feedback is immediateEvery action gets a response within a frame people can trust.
  • 03
    Restraint is a decisionThe best interaction is often the one you don't notice at all.
